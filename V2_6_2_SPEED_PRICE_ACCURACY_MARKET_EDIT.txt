NUNES AI CRM V2.6.2

Changes only in product/price intelligence:
- India / Export pricing type is editable directly in Price Information.
- India margin policy: default 30%, normal 30-40%.
- Export margin policy: default 30%, normal 30-60%.
- Saved complete product records are reused instead of calling Gemini again.
- New product generation uses one fast grounded Gemini synthesis path by default.
- New-lead product generation and online price research start in parallel.
- Search Price Again refreshes PRICE ONLY and no longer waits for product regeneration.
- IndiaMART and Gemini Google Search price discovery now run in parallel with strict exact-model checks.
- Price candidates without a verifiable supporting URL are skipped.
- Online search timeouts/variant counts reduced to keep the form responsive.
- Original / Online Price no longer falls back to historical selling/purchase values.
- Existing Company CRM, WhatsApp/Gmail budget request, quotation and other functions remain unchanged.
