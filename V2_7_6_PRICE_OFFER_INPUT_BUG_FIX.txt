V2.7.6 PRICE / LIMITED-OFFER INPUT BUG FIX

- Fixed the grey/non-editable numeric fields shown in Price Information and Limited-Time Offer.
- Online Price is editable. If exact online research is unavailable, staff can enter the verified/base price manually.
- Manual Online Price is stored safely as a Manual Price Entry and is reused after refresh/restart.
- Margin % is directly editable within the INDIA / EXPORT allowed range; the pencil icon now focuses the field instead of locking/unlocking it.
- Final Selling Price is editable and remains synchronized with Online Price + Margin when those inputs change.
- Limited-Time Offer Validity, Offer Discount, Regular Selling Price and Limited Offer Price are all editable.
- No Limited-Time Offer numeric field is disabled just because online research did not find a price.
- Safe Margin Protection remains enforced on the server. If staff enters an unsafe discount/offer price, the server clamps it to the minimum approved margin instead of allowing a loss.
- Existing CRM, dashboard, staff photos, Gmail, WhatsApp, Gemini, quotations and old data behavior are unchanged.
