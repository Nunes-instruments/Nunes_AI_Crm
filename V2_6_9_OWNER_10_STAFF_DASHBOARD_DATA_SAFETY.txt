NUNES AI CRM V2.6.9

Added only the requested owner/team functions on top of V2.6.8.

- Owner dashboard for 10 staff members.
- Staff photo/name/designation profiles.
- Per-staff Today / Week / Month form completion, open enquiries, waiting product/price, orders and won value.
- Daily 7-day and weekly 8-week completion graphs.
- Click any staff card for the full staff enquiry/form/order report.
- Staff device profile shows only that staff member's assigned enquiries and quotations in normal UI.
- Owner view shows all staff and main reports.
- Final form save is stored permanently with completion time and 100% completion status.
- SQLite WAL persistence retained. Automatic database safety backup runs after startup and every 6 hours; up to 30 recent automatic backups are kept.
- CREATE_STAFF_DESKTOP_SHORTCUT.bat creates an easy desktop launcher for another staff PC without installing the CRM server on that PC.
- Existing CRM, Gemini, price, margins, offer, Gmail/WhatsApp and sales-assistant functions remain unchanged.
