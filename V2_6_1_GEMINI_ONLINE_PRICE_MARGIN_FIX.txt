NUNES AI CRM V2.6.1

CHANGED ONLY THE REQUESTED PRODUCT/PRICE INTELLIGENCE AREA.

1. Gemini product generation
- Server-side Gemini key is already included in this build.
- Uses current Gemini Flash model with automatic fallback models.
- Product Name remains the source of truth.
- Generates Specifications, Description, Key Features and Applications.
- Generated product information is stored/reused for repeated enquiries.
- The form shows the safe Gemini failure reason if generation fails.

2. Exact online price research
- Searches IndiaMART first for the exact product/model.
- Falls back to Gemini grounded online search when needed.
- Never accepts a similar model when an exact model was requested.
- If confidence is insufficient, no price is guessed; the form shows why the online price was skipped.
- GST, supplier and stock are filled only when available from matched evidence.

3. Price form simplified
Removed from the visible price form:
- Last Purchase Price
- Last Selling Price
- Previous Quoted Price
- Lead Time
- Source input

Visible price fields:
- Original / Online Price
- Margin %
- Selling Price
- GST
- Supplier
- Price Date
- Stock
- Price Verified

4. India / Export pricing
- Default margin: 30%
- INDIA normal range: 30% to 40%
- EXPORT normal range: 30% to 60%
- Margin remains editable. Selling Price recalculates from the original/reference price.

All other CRM functions remain unchanged, including Company CRM, product library reuse, WhatsApp/Gmail budget request, quotations and follow-ups.
